# FitNexx — Market Requirements Document

## Market problem

Most fitness apps and training templates prescribe a fixed plan and leave progress tracking to the user. Lifters who want their training to genuinely adapt to performance — rather than just log it after the fact — are underserved by both generic fitness apps (broad, not strength-specific) and static program templates (no feedback loop).

## Target market

Individual lifters running structured strength, hybrid, or conditioning/mobility programs, particularly those currently using a spreadsheet or fixed PDF/template program and wanting the plan itself to react to their logged numbers.

## Why now

Strength training has moved mainstream alongside a broader data-tracking culture (wearables, fitness apps), but most of that tracking is descriptive (it shows you what happened) rather than prescriptive (it doesn't change what you do next). FitNexx is positioned in that gap: a plan that changes because of what you logged.

## Competitive landscape

- **Generic fitness/workout logging apps** — good at logging, but largely descriptive; the program itself typically doesn't adapt
- **Fixed program templates/PDFs** — well-designed programming, but zero feedback loop once purchased
- **Personal coaching** — genuinely adaptive, but expensive and not scalable to a self-serve product

## Value proposition

FitNexx logs a lifter's working sets and adjusts the next session based on what was actually hit — giving structured programming with a feedback loop, without the cost of a personal coach.

## Positioning

A self-serve strength platform positioned between static program templates and personal coaching — adaptive programming without the coaching price tag.

## Status

Live v1 with a defined pricing page; not yet validated with paying subscribers at scale.
